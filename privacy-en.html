Shen Xuankong · Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Effective: November 17, 2026

“Shen Xuankong” (the “App”) is operated by Liangjiang New Area Jiayin Nian Software Development Studio (sole proprietorship) (the “Developer”). We take personal information seriously. This policy explains what we collect, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have. A Chinese version is available at the Chinese page.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address or mobile number you provide when registering.
  • Chart input: birth date, time and place that you enter. These are used only to generate your own charts, for no other purpose.
  • Content you create: saved charts, environmental annotations, notes and site layouts, stored under your account.
  • Purchase information: transaction receipts generated by Apple In-App Purchase, used to verify delivery and grant entitlements.

We do not collect: precise location (GPS), contacts, photo library, clipboard contents, or advertising identifiers. The App contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.

2. How we use information

We use the information above only to: provide sign-in and chart casting, store your records, verify purchases, and deliver SMS verification codes. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or cross-app tracking, and we do not sell it to any third party.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored with a security-certified cloud provider (Supabase; data centres located outside mainland China). All traffic uses HTTPS. SMS verification is provided by Tencent Cloud, which receives only the mobile number and the verification content needed to send the message.

4. Retention and deletion

You may delete your account at any time in the App under Me → Account → Delete Account. Deletion immediately removes your account information and all associated data and cannot be undone. You may also request deletion by emailing 714192727@qq.com.

5. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

  • Subscriptions renew automatically. The next period is charged within 24 hours before the current period ends, and you can cancel at any time in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Credits are consumable virtual items used to unlock specific content. They are non-transferable and non-refundable except where required by law.
  • Subscription terms are governed by Apple’s standard End User License Agreement (EULA): https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Content disclaimer

All content is compiled from traditional Xuankong (Flying Star) theory and classical texts. It is provided for the study of traditional culture and for personal record-keeping only. It does not constitute medical, investment, legal or any other professional advice, and no outcome is promised.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will announce them in the App and update the “Last updated” date on this page.
